Taco Trivia

When did tacos first appear in the United States?

Tacos first appeared in the United States in the early 20th century.
They were introduced by Mexican immigrants in states like Texas and California.
Initially, tacos were sold at small food stands and local markets.
They became widely popular in the U.S.
Source: About Tacos
